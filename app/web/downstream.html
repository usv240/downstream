The Collaborative Partner

A plan no owner should have to write alone.

Give Downstream a dam and it works on its own: it resolves the public record, reads the legacy drawing, notices when two sources disagree, drafts every section it has evidence for, refuses the map it cannot justify, and schedules its own follow-ups. Then it stops and asks the owner the handful of things only they know — and keeps working between sessions, for weeks, without being opened.

Run the guided demoFollow the judge pathGet an API key

The public workspace needs no key at all. A developer key takes about thirty seconds — self-service, no invitation, with a browser console and full reference.

Boundary: this demo never generates an inundation boundary, evacuation zone, certification, or submission. It produces a synthetic draft and a documented safe stop.

The friction

The form is not the hard part.

Facts are scattered across public records, old drawings, local memory, and jurisdiction-specific guidance.

01 Runs itself

Resolve everything it can

One trigger. It reads the federal record and the drawing, grounds every fact in a quote, and derives the conflict between them. No clicks.

02 Stops itself

Ask only what it must not invent

Owner knowledge is the one input it is not allowed to guess. It asks one question at a time, and says why it is asking.

03 Keeps working

Between sessions, unattended

Durable wakes reopen questions held for later and follow up on a stalled draft. Cloud Scheduler runs them whether or not anyone is watching.

04 Shows its work

A receipt, not a claim

Every step is on an ordered timeline with the actor that performed it, so the autonomy is counted from stored state rather than asserted.
