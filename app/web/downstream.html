The Collaborative Partner

A plan no owner should have to write alone.

Downstream combines public dam records, reviewable document facts, and the knowledge only an owner has. It asks one useful question at a time, remembers across sessions, and assembles a cited Emergency Action Plan draft for qualified review.

Open the judge presetOpen the evidence dashboard

Boundary: this demo never generates an inundation boundary, evacuation zone, certification, or submission. It produces a synthetic draft and a documented safe stop.
