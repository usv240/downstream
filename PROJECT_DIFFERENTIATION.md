# Project differentiation

Downstream is the third independent submission and targets The Collaborative Partner.

| Dimension | Downstream | Day Three | Sixty Days |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary user | Resource-constrained dam owner | Hospital antimicrobial stewardship team | Disaster-assistance applicant |
| Category | Collaborative Partner | Fortified Enterprise Fleet | Taskmaster |
| Time shape | Many short authoring sessions | Asynchronous institutional monitoring | Deadline-bound evidence workflow |
| Main output | Reviewable Emergency Action Plan draft | Verified clinical coordination artifacts | Reviewable appeal packet draft |
| Adaptation | Language, vocabulary, detail, and session style | Enterprise policy and agent routing | Evidence and deadline state |
| Safety stop | No unvalidated inundation boundary | No unsourced clinical claim or autonomous clinical action | No autonomous send or submission |

The repositories share a reviewed infrastructure spine. Their domain agents, fixtures, user
journeys, data, interfaces, categories, outputs, tests, and deployments are substantially different.
